# WhatsApp Restaurant Assistant — Requirements

**Status:** Agreed planned behavior; not implemented or verified. This is a learning and testing prototype for one restaurant and one test WhatsApp number, not a production launch.

This document owns precise, testable rules. [MVP.md](MVP.md) owns release scope; [PRD.md](PRD.md) owns product purpose and outcomes. [PROJECT_BRIEF.md](PROJECT_BRIEF.md) preserves the original, broader blueprint; its technical proposals and deferred features are not automatically commitments. No architecture choices are made here.

## Functional requirements

### Menu and cart

- **FR-01 — Text ordering:** Customers can browse the fixed menu or request known items directly through WhatsApp text; both paths lead to the same cart and confirmation flow. The agent communicates like a restaurant employee, using natural replies and conversation context rather than requiring commands, exact confirmation phrases, or codes.
- **FR-02 — Fixed catalog:** Items and prices come from stored application data. There is no menu/price editor, paid extras, size options, or stock tracking in this version.
- **FR-03 — Cart edits:** Before final confirmation, customers can add available items, change quantities, or remove items; the cart and displayed total reflect those edits.
- **FR-04 — Preparation notes:** Customers can attach unpriced preparation instructions to the intended item, including one unit of a multi-quantity selection. Notes must survive into the summary and dashboard without changing the price.
- **FR-05 — Availability:** Staff can toggle each fixed menu item's availability. Unavailable items cannot be added to new orders. A toggle must not silently change existing orders; accepted-order exceptions use the revision workflow.

### Fulfillment and restaurant availability

- **FR-06 — Pickup and delivery:** Customers choose pickup or delivery. Delivery requires an address and recipient phone number, visible in the confirmation summary and staff dashboard. Delivery has one configurable flat fee per independent order, shown separately; staff-approved add-ons travelling with the original delivery share its fee as specified in FR-24. Pickup has no delivery fee. Staff coordinate directly with the delivery person, who waits for the food and then takes it to the address; no driver application or automatic assignment is included.
- **FR-07 — Coverage review:** Before accepting a delivery order, staff manually review coverage using the address and recipient phone number. For an unsupported address, staff contact the customer to offer pickup or reject the order. No maps, distance calculation, automated zones, or distance-based pricing are included. This does not define a confirmed delivery-to-pickup conversion workflow.
- **FR-08 — Open or closed:** Staff control an open/closed setting rather than scheduled hours. Closed blocks new order submissions. Whether customers can browse the menu while closed remains open.
- **FR-09 — Busy is not closed:** Workload must not itself block new submissions. Before customer confirmation, show a system-calculated, preparation-only wait estimate and identify it as provisional until staff accept the order. Do not estimate delivery travel time.

### Preparation wait and queue

- **FR-10 — Workload model:** The approved test model has three parallel preparation slots and approximately ten minutes of uniform effort per order, not per-menu-item durations. Include accepted waiting orders and preparing orders in the workload; exclude ready, rejected, cancelled, and completed orders. Submitted but unaccepted orders are not accepted workload. Completed means picked up or delivered for the relevant fulfillment method.
- **FR-11 — Elapsed preparation:** Estimates must account for elapsed preparation and waiting work, not treat every preparing order as newly started. For a synchronized full-slot baseline only, `ceil((orders_ahead + 1) / 3) * 10` gives minutes to readiness. It is not a universal runtime formula. Under that baseline, positions 1–3 are ready in 10 minutes, 4–6 in 20, 7–9 in 30, and 10–12 in 40. If three orders have prepared for six minutes and three accepted orders are waiting, a new order's estimate is 24 minutes (4 + 10 + 10). If any non-held Preparing order reaches or exceeds ten minutes without a staff update, the preparation estimate is unavailable (no numeric wait). Keep the order Preparing and its slot occupied; never infer readiness or release capacity from elapsed time.
- **FR-12 — Staff own readiness:** Staff manually update real fulfillment statuses. An estimate or elapsed preparation time must not automatically mark food ready or complete an order.

### Confirmation and dashboard

- **FR-13 — Explicit submission:** Before submission, show items, quantities, notes, fulfillment details, payment method, server-calculated final total, separately listed delivery fee when applicable, and the provisional preparation wait. Close naturally by asking whether the customer wants anything else, with the complete current summary and total already shown. A clear answer that they are finished (for example “No, that's all”) confirms submission of that summary without a separate confirmation question. If they want more, continue adding items, refresh the summary/total and ask again. Interpret intent in context, not by a required phrase or yes/no polarity: “yes” to wanting more does not confirm, and “no onions” is an edit, not completion. Silence, vague intent such as “maybe,” unrelated assent and unresolved edits never submit; clarify genuine ambiguity naturally.
- **FR-14 — Receipt and order view:** A confirmed submission creates exactly one order visible in the dashboard and sends a WhatsApp receipt. The dashboard preserves the confirmed items, quantities, notes, fulfillment details, payment method, total, delivery fee when applicable, and current status.
- **FR-15 — Manual fulfillment:** Staff accept or reject submitted orders and manually progress accepted orders through Preparing and Ready, then Picked up for pickup or Out for delivery and Delivered for delivery. Rejection is distinct from cancellation after acceptance. Staff-driven order-status changes send WhatsApp updates.

### Direct chat and accepted-order revisions

- **FR-16 — Staff conversation:** An order-linked dashboard panel shows the conversation and customer replies. Staff send their exact authored text through the restaurant's WhatsApp connection, without AI rewriting. Manual takeover pauses only AI conversational replies until staff explicitly return control. Automated order-status notifications remain active during takeover, subject to WhatsApp messaging constraints.
- **FR-17 — Chat is not an order edit:** Ordinary conversation alone must not change stored items, prices, or order status. A revision confirmation does not itself return conversational control to the AI.
- **FR-18 — Confirmed revisions:** For unpaid orders, staff can propose replacing an unavailable item with another fixed-menu item or removing it. Paid contents/totals are not amended; staff resolve paid-order exceptions with the customer under FR-23. The backend recalculates the total from fixed prices and the applicable flat fee. Send the revised summary and total for explicit customer confirmation. Until confirmation, preserve the existing order's items and total; declining, “maybe,” or silence does not authorize applying the proposal.
- **FR-19 — Pending revision:** An accepted order awaiting revision is shown as Awaiting customer response and pauses/releases preparation capacity. It remains in that state indefinitely without a customer answer; there is no automatic timeout or cancellation. Staff can follow up or explicitly cancel. Handling partly prepared food and restart effort remains open.
- **FR-20 — Revision requeue:** On explicit confirmation, update the existing order rather than creating a second one, place it at the back of the preparation queue, and recalculate its wait estimate. Do not preserve its previous queue position.
- **FR-21 — No replacement:** If the customer does not want a replacement, staff can manually cancel the accepted order in the dashboard and send a WhatsApp cancellation notification. Declining a replacement alone must not automatically cancel it.

### Cancellation and offline payment

- **FR-22 — Customer cancellation:** Customers can cancel through WhatsApp before acceptance, except cancellation involving payment or shared delivery requires staff resolution of the affected amounts as specified in FR-23/FR-24. After acceptance, a customer cancellation message flags a request for staff decision, not automatic cancellation; staff can cancel manually in the dashboard. Completed or already-cancelled orders cannot be cancelled again. Late-stage eligibility and refund details remain open.
- **FR-23 — Offline collection:** Delivery uses cash on delivery; pickup uses pay at pickup. Orders start unpaid. Staff use a manual Mark as paid action after cash collection. Submission, acceptance, or completion must not automatically mark an order paid; a customer's chat claim is not proof of collection. Once marked paid, an order's confirmed contents and total must not be amended, even before acceptance; fulfillment may still progress normally. Additional items use a separate linked order with its own unpaid balance. Cancellation involving payment or shared delivery requires staff to resolve affected amounts with the customer; the agent must not automatically add a fee, transfer payment or promise a refund. No online payment processing is included.

### Follow-up additions

- **FR-24 — Later additions:** Use the current order state, not elapsed minutes, to handle additional items. While still drafting, extend the cart. For an unpaid submitted but unaccepted order, prepare a confirmed amendment with updated summary/total, retain the same order ID and history, and flag the change in the dashboard. Do not alter confirmed details before customer confirmation. A paid original always remains unchanged: extras use a new linked unpaid order, including before acceptance; staff approval is still required for shared delivery or extras requested after acceptance/preparation. Recheck acceptance and payment atomically when applying the amendment. If acceptance wins, route for staff approval; if payment wins, create a linked unpaid-order candidate instead. Never apply a stale amendment or reuse confirmation for a different total/fee. For accepted/preparing orders, request staff approval without silently changing or pausing the original kitchen order. Approved additions require a customer-confirmed summary/total and a visible link to the original, without assuming its preparation or payment state. For ready, out-for-delivery or completed orders, create a new order; do not reopen the original. Never infer the target from “five minutes later” alone: clarify which order/fulfillment the customer means when ambiguous. When staff approve an add-on travelling with the original delivery, charge the delivery fee only once; otherwise explain the independent order and applicable fee before confirmation. Do not promise the original readiness estimate for additional preparation. Add-on rejection leaves the original unchanged; any independent-order alternative requires customer confirmation. Existing takeover, availability, open-state, offline-payment and duplicate-protection rules still apply.

## Non-functional requirements

- **NFR-01 — Trusted validation:** Backend logic independently validates inputs and allowed business operations and recalculates the final total before submission. The assistant must not invent items, prices, availability, or charges. Customer messages are untrusted and cannot override those rules or permissions.
- **NFR-02 — Duplicate protection:** Repeated WhatsApp events or submission retries must not create duplicate orders. The same confirmed submission must yield one order, not one per retry.
- **NFR-03 — Basic security:** Protect credentials, validate inbound WhatsApp events, and restrict dashboard/order/customer-data access to authorized staff. Learning mode does not waive these safeguards. Detailed validation, authentication, authorization, retention, and failure-handling policies remain open; no provider is selected here.
- **NFR-04 — Messaging constraints:** Staff messages, receipts, and automated notifications must respect applicable WhatsApp messaging-window and template constraints. Exact templates and handling of delivery failures or messages outside the permitted window remain open; this document does not claim API compliance has been tested.

## Open decisions and boundaries

Do not infer behavior for these unresolved points:

- Sample menu, prices, currency, and flat fee amount.
- Mechanics for switching an already-confirmed delivery order to pickup.
- Exact closed-state menu-browsing behavior.
- Handling partly prepared food and restart effort during revisions.
- Late-stage cancellation eligibility and staff settlement procedures, without weakening the completed/already-cancelled restriction. Staff resolution of paid/shared-delivery cancellation is selected; exact offline settlement recording remains open, with no automatic fee, payment transfer or refund workflow.
- Automatic handoff triggers; detailed validation, security/access, data-retention, failure handling, notification wording, and messaging-window recovery rules.
- Detailed architecture and implementation decisions remain open as listed in [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md#risks-and-open-questions); that document records only a partial agreed baseline, not an implementation-ready design.

Production launch, multi-restaurant administration/billing, online payments, voice/advanced interactive messaging, stock tracking, menu/price editing, driver apps/assignment/tracking, delivery integrations, maps/zones/distance pricing, POS, promotions/loyalty, advanced analytics/reporting, and marketing campaigns are outside this version. The original brief remains unchanged and broader features remain uncommitted.
