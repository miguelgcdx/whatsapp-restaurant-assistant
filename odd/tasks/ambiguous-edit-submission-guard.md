Separate from prior pickup fixture/core verification outcomes. Preserve concurrent work and existing no-ref/publishing restrictions.

S4. AI boundary clarification: "not_complete means 'do not submit this assessment', NOT 'persist ambiguity', 'resolution' or 'abandonment'. A later complete does NOT itself authorize clearing a backend ambiguity guard. No confidence/ready flag or model-owned ID/version/policy authority."
S5. AI boundary clarification: "cartintent strictly decodes Add/Remove/SetNote proposals for a trusted cart; it is not an ambiguity lifecycle/confirmation authority. Invalid/malformed proposal returns zero call+ErrInput, not an implicit unresolved marker. A successfully applied concrete edit changes trusted cart/version, but no approved AI contract currently correlates that to resolving a persisted ambiguity record. Do not infer abandonment from not_complete, failed decode or unrelated assent."
