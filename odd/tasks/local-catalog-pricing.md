### S3 — Pure unitized draft pricing from trusted fixed catalog

Preserve distinct notes/IDs and input slices (no mutation/aliasing) on successful or rejected add/remove/note changes; remove targets exact unit ID, never implicit quantity reduction. Reject unknown/unavailable additions, missing/duplicate units/IDs, invalid UTF-8, negative amounts, mismatched currencies and checked-int64 overflow. Availability changes cannot silently remove/reprice already present draft units; quote from the trusted catalog, and reject invalid stored catalog rather than shorten content. No arbitrary order-size business cap. Fulfillment is pickup (zero fee/pay_at_pickup, no delivery fields) or delivery (explicit nonblank address<=1000 code points and phone<=128; cash_on_delivery, configured fee exactly once); no maps/coverage claims. Draft without selected fulfillment may exist, but final Quote requires explicit valid fulfillment. Subtotal=sum per-unit fixed prices, total=subtotal+fee, no float arithmetic. Empty pure draft may total zero, but this grants no right to submit an empty order.
